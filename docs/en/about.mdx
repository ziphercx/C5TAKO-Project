# Meet zipher

zipher brings together tools and knowledge for **cybersecurity, hardware, and system testing** for people who want to learn, experiment, and develop their skills.

We design devices, firmware, documentation, and community channels that work together, from first learning about a device to experimenting in a controlled environment.

<Info>

Use zipher tools only with your own devices or systems for which you have explicit authorization.

</Info>

## Use responsibly

zipher makes tools for both offensive and defensive security testing, learning, and research. Use them only on systems, devices, or networks you own or are explicitly authorized to test, and check applicable law and system policies before every test.

<Warning>

Do not use these devices or commands to attack, disrupt, access, intercept data from, or test another person's system without permission, including any form of cyber abuse or harassment. You are solely responsible for your use. zipher and its developers are not responsible for damage or consequences arising from unauthorized, illegal, or unintended use.

</Warning>
